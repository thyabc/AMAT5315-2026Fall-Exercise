\# Tutor Skill



\## Purpose



Teach a lesson from a local file or PDF step by step.





\## Procedure



1\. Read the lesson file.

2\. Explain one concept at a time.

3\. Ask the learner if they are ready.

4\. Continue only after confirmation.

5\. Ask checkpoint questions at the end.





\## Rules



Do not skip steps.

Do not declare success if the checkpoint answer is wrong.
